## Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication (CC0-1.0)

Provider is, voluntarily and without financial compensation, providing gridded bathymetric data to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) under a Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal public domain dedication (CC0-1.0).

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Database Integration | NOAA intends to register and integrate Provider's Data into the Office of Coast Survey source database. |
| Derivative Products | Creation of electronic navigational charts, mesh generated products (flooding, tsunami, VDATUM), and precision navigation (S-102). |
| Mission Support | Hydrographic survey planning and hydrographic data gap analysis for Seabed 2030 initiatives. |
| Public Release | Products and original data will be publicly released with no restrictions on use. |

Furthermore, NOAA intends to publicly release the Provider's Data in its original form under the same CC0-1.0 public domain dedication ([https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). ## Internal Use Only

Provider is, voluntarily and without financial compensation, providing gridded bathymetric data to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). The data will be for internal use only by NOAA and its contractors and affiliates, to the extent permitted under Federal laws and regulations.

| Constraint | Policy |
|---|---|
| Database Integration | NOAA will not integrate Provider's Data into the Office of Coast Survey's source database. |
| Derivative Restrictions | NOAA will not create derivative products such as navigational charts, mesh products, or S-102 products. |
| Authorized Use | Internal NOAA purposes only, including hydrographic survey planning and Seabed 2030 data gap analysis. |
